new sanctions:

bottom line

New sanctions are a little less likely than not in the next 60 days. If they come, we would have about 30 days to stop dealing with two resin suppliers, three Dubai traders and two feeder ships, and $62M of value over the next year would be at risk.

Two moves pay off whether or not sanctions arrive: qualify non-Gulf resin for Gebze and line up a second bank for Gebze's payments. Start both this month. Hold the costlier move, pausing the Dubai traders, until a tripwire trips.

  1. moderate confidence

    The Administration is more likely to widen today's Iran sanctions than to write new ones, so buyers and shippers of Iranian petrochemicals would be hit first.

  2. high confidence

    Our biggest exposure is resin, not sales: two Gulf suppliers that take feedstock from an Iranian-linked joint venture carry 38% of Gebze's resin.

  3. low confidence

    Any wind-down window is likely to be 30 days or shorter. New resin takes 6 to 8 weeks to qualify, so waiting until the announcement is too late.
