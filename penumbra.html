one question,
three engines.

  1. Penumbra's Totality engine takes the question apart.

    Real questions are tangled. (What does “ceasefire in Ukraine” really mean?) The Totality engine divides each query into parts that can actually be forecast. No other forecaster in the field decomposes questions this way.

  2. It gets each subquery to the right engine.

    A union's choice, a plant's output and a contract deadline move in different ways. Inside Totality are three engines: one for judgment, one for telemetry, one for procedure. Each knows how far ahead it can see. Most other forecasters run one kind of model on every question.

  3. It fuses the answers into actionable intelligence.

    Averaging good forecasts makes a worse one. Totality's fusion layer combines all three engines into a single calibrated probability, with error bars.
